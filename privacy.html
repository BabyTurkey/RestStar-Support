Information We Collect

RestStar may collect account information such as names, usernames, restaurant associations, employee roles, training activity, quiz results, certifications, and other information necessary to provide restaurant training services.

How We Use Information

Information is used to provide account access, restaurant training, employee progress tracking, quizzes, certifications, menu training, and administrative features.

Data Storage

RestStar uses cloud infrastructure and third-party service providers to securely store and process application data.

Data Sharing

RestStar does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only as necessary to operate and support the service, or where required by law.

Data Retention and Deletion

Restaurant administrators may manage employee accounts and associated training information. Data may be retained as necessary to operate the service, meet legal requirements, and maintain platform security.
